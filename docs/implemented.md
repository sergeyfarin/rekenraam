backend and UI status tracked separately. It replaces the per-step trackers
(`transaction-table-plan.md`, `phase-1-implementation-plan.md`,

## Provider independence policy — 2026-10-15

[ADR 0017](adrs/0017-provider-independent-features-and-incomplete-data.md)
records the owner's direction: provider gaps do not gate manual/file features;
optional attribution may stay unresolved with disclosure and repair. This is a
policy/planning change, not new runtime functionality. Existing manual commands,
CSV input, unknown-basis resolution and manual price/FX management remain the
shipped foundations. Known-payer fallback review/repair now ships under #242
and manual short-operation import linking under #245, and dividend attribution
coverage under #244 (all below).

## Statement disclosure of unclassified imported dividend cash — #246 (2026-10-16)

Each R18 period statement currency row now discloses the count and exact cash
of in-period committed Trading 212 dividends still posted as ordinary cash
(unknown gross and withholding), beside income and never in income, the
limited-scope total or the reporting-currency figures. Review through #242
moves a payment into income at its gross. New saved statements are schema 3
(migration 0010 preserves earlier bytes and hashes); schema-1/2 statements say
the disclosure is unavailable. Six locales, 390 px, linked to income coverage.
See the [policy](plans/investment-reporting-plan.md#unclassified-imported-dividend-cash-246).
Named evidence: `TestGainsStatementDisclosesUnclassifiedImportedDividendCash`
and `income-coverage.spec.ts`.

## Dividend income attribution coverage — #244 (2026-10-15)

`GET /api/v1/investments/income-coverage` composes owner-confirmed dividend
income per paying instrument and cash currency (gross, withholding, net cash;
no recorded income, known zero and known tax stay distinct) beside the book's
identifiable unassigned investment cash in its original currencies. Unassigned
items are named dividends without a payer and committed Trading 212 dividend
sources still posted as ordinary cash, identified only through operation facts
and import provenance. Their cash is exact; unknown gross and withholding are
reported as unresolved, never as income or zero. A source ISIN/ticker that
names an existing instrument is disclosed beside it as unclassified cash and
never added. Reads never write; items page by cursor while totals cover all.

A phone-ready coverage page (linked from Investments) and an instrument income
summary show these states, the book-wide excluded scope, raw source evidence and
optional repair links: #242's same-payment review for imported cash, or the
dividend correction form, which can now assign a payer to a payer-less dividend.
Six locales; loading, empty, error and success states. No schema, export or
report-total change. See the [contract](plans/dividend-income-coverage-plan.md).

Named evidence: `TestDividendCoverageSeparatesKnownUnassignedAndCashOnlyIncome`,
`TestDividendCoverageCursorPagesUnassignedItemsWithoutGapsOrDuplicates`, API
`TestIncomeCoverageAPIDisclosesCashOnlyImportAndFollowsRepair`; browser
`income-coverage.spec.ts` (390 px, axe, named payer correction, imported
cash disclosure and repair link).

## Manual short-operation import links — #245 (2026-10-15)

A pending Trading 212 fill whose type is not a documented `TRADE` (and is not a
`STOCK_SPLIT`, which keeps its own link) never posts and is never treated as a
short by itself. Import review now offers **Link to recorded short**:
`GET /api/v1/imports/{batch}/rows/{row}/short-candidates` returns the row's raw
evidence and the effective recorded short sales (SELL row) or covers (BUY row) of
its security — looked up, never created — each with the known source facts that
disagree with it (date, units, signed net cash, cash currency, the connection's
mapped holding account and cash account). `POST …/link-short` admits the row's
dedupe identity with the chosen operation as its only effect and marks the row
committed under one audit event (`import.row.link_short_sale` /
`import.row.link_short_cover`); no journal, lot or disposal is written. Any
mismatch refuses (`IMPORT_SHORT_LINK_MISMATCH`); an already-linked operation
(including through a correction lineage), another side or security, a
committed/duplicate row or a discarded batch is `IMPORT_SHORT_LINK_UNAVAILABLE`;
an operation corrected after review is `IMPORT_SHORT_LINK_CHANGED`. The writer
re-reads the row, batch and every reviewed operation fact inside its
transaction, and the unique effect index settles concurrent links. Reimport is a
duplicate; native replace/reverse keeps the identity on the immutable original,
the replacement inherits it through its lineage, and raw provider evidence is
never rewritten. The split link and the short link share one admission writer.
No schema migration. Automatic provider short admission stays #176. Mobile
review in six locales shows the provider evidence, disables mismatched or
linked candidates with the reason, and covers loading, empty, error and success.

`TestTrading212ShortLinkSurvivesNativeCorrection`,
`TestTrading212ShortLinkRollsBackOnLateFailure`,

## Cash dividend instrument attribution — #229 (2026-10-15)

Cash dividends store an optional immutable paying-instrument fact on the named
operation, shared by gross income and withholding. Separate withholding-tax
payments require an instrument and record only tax and cash, without gross
income or security units; entry, correction and reversal are available in all
six locales, including the instrument view. Instrument activity includes
the complete cash journal, replacements and reversals in its existing paginated
read. Corrections expose and retain the payer when omitted, including older API
clients; explicitly correcting the payer assigns only the replacement to the new
instrument and keeps the inverse with the predecessor. Trading 212's native
import retains its resolved instrument atomically with the journal and source
identity. Bundle schema 13 carries `investment-dividend-facts.csv`; migration 9
preserves existing journals and leaves historical unidentified payers unassigned.
Explicit CSV dividend/tax mapping ships under #241 (below). Trading 212's
missing-default payer loss and historical fallback recovery are fixed by #242
(below), completing #229's imported-attribution scope.
Re-reconciliation after checkpoint invalidation shipped separately under
[#240](https://github.com/sergeyfarin/rekenraam/issues/240) (Reconciliation, below).

## Imported dividend review and same-payment repair — #242 (2026-10-15)

`/app/import/dividends` exposes a composed paginated queue of pending Trading 212
dividends and historical ordinary-cash fallbacks, reachable from import preview,
results and the upload screen. Owners confirm gross/withholding and accounts
without a provider refresh; local defaults are optional. New dividends never
fall back to ordinary cash or infer gross/zero tax from source cash. Missing
required facts keep only that row pending; other rows can commit. A payer is
resolved from recorded source identity, explicitly selected, or left unassigned
when no identity resolves. Instrument setup happens only at commit, with cleanup
after failed writes and retry support even for older failed batches.

Historical repair pins the current version and source eligibility inside one
writer, appends a named dividend version to the same transaction ID, and records
an operation-only import effect beside the original immutable transaction effect.
It preserves exact source cash/date/currency/account, owner metadata, line keys,
ordering and unchanged reconciliation. Reallocated reconciled legs require
explicit override. Audit, facts, source effects and batch state commit together;
stale/discarded review and late failures roll back. Native correction/reversal,
instrument activity, export bundle 13 and reimport dedupe reuse the existing
operation model. No schema migration or guessed historical backfill. All six
locales and the 390px workflow include loading, empty, error and success states.
Wider attribution coverage is delivered by #244. See the
[contract](plans/import-dividend-review-plan.md).

## CSV dividend and withholding mapping — #241 (2026-10-15)

Saved CSV profiles can opt into explicit dividend, separate withholding or mixed
row kinds, exact existing instrument commodity codes, independent gross/tax
columns and cash/income/tax account defaults. Signed cash is never assumed gross;
all amount columns use one file-level separator and exact gross minus tax must
match cash. Unknown kinds/instruments, invalid source cash and inconsistent
amounts require review before any row commits. The phone review can select
instrument, kind, accounts, cash currency and localized gross/tax amounts; one
catalog request serves all rows. Bank-only profiles and preview rules retain
their existing behavior. Investment rows do not apply bank categorization rules.

The shared named dividend writer records gross and combined tax, or cash/tax
only for separate withholding. Journal, operation/payer facts, source identity
and row result commit atomically; reimport deduplicates and native audited
correction/reversal and bundle schema 13 reuse #229. Reconciliation preview is
read-only; refusal keeps the batch reviewable. An accepted batch retains the
existing per-row commit/rollback contract. No implicit instruments, guessed
provider semantics or schema migration. See the
[contract](plans/csv-investment-income-plan.md).
